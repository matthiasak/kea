The kea and the snow

The kea is a large parrot of the alpine forests and high country of the South Island of New Zealand. Olive green with a brilliant orange under its wings, it is known for its intelligence and its curiosity, which takes it into tents, cars and the pockets of anyone who stops for long enough.

Kea live in family groups through the winter, feeding on roots, leaves, berries and insects. They play in the snow, slide down roofs and work through puzzles that would defeat most other birds, and young kea learn by watching the older birds of their group.

They nest on the ground among the roots of trees and in rock crevices, and a pair raises two to four chicks. Numbers fell sharply in the last century, and the bird is now protected, with work under way to keep stoats and possums away from the nests.
